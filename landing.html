RESQNET

An IoT-Based School Assistance Alert and Response Monitoring System

A school assistance system designed to help students and personnel request assistance through registered alert stations and notify authorized school personnel.

📡

IoT Alert Stations

ESP32-based stations send assistance alerts through Wi-Fi.

📍

Device-Based Location

Each alert is identified using a registered Device ID and assigned school location.

🚨

Alert Monitoring

Authorized personnel can monitor and respond to assistance requests.

Prototype Coverage: HEB • GZB • TSB • ABB • Gym
